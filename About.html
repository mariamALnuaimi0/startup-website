Overview

Founded in 1988, the Higher Colleges of Technology (HCT) is the UAE’s largest applied higher education institution and the nation’s first approved university economic free zone, empowering it to graduate companies in alignment with the directives of the UAE’s wise leadership. Renowned as a dynamic hub of technology-driven innovation and excellence in education, HCT has gained an enviable reputation for its agile, innovative, and job-focused practices, fulfilling its mission of igniting Emirati excellence.


Over 25,000 students pursue their studies at state-of-the-art, technology-driven campuses in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Al Dhafra, Dubai, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah. Through long-standing and productive partnerships with industry, government, and academia, HCT has fostered strong local and international collaborations, as reflected in the achievement of its strategic objectives. HCT proudly celebrates the accomplishments of its 80,000+ graduates, who, through their performance and career progression, demonstrate the outstanding education they received—rooted in application, innovation, and the ability to adapt to changes and challenges in the labour market.


HCT’s new strategy focuses on positioning the institution as a leader in applied sciences, showcasing Emirati excellence locally and globally. With a vision of excellence and a mission to offer applied education and research in collaboration with industry, the strategy is built on core values of honor, courage, and trust. It emphasizes three strategic pillars: Talent Engine, Catalyst for Transformation, and Thriving Community. The main objectives are to provide a best-in-class student experience, enhance graduate employability, and foster a vibrant, supportive community.
